We named the team after Eugene Shoemaker, who founded USGS Astrogeology and trained Apollo astronauts in geology at analog sites like Meteor Crater.

We picked the look-alikes for their rock, so terrain failing is the finding.

Beyond the usual sites, the best mare matches are Arabia's basalt fields.

Karacadağ in Türkiye matches on rock too (rank 11 of 22,030) but lies under farmland: a place to sample, not to drive.

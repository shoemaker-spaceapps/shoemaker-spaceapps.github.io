windows of 10 km across Earth. Which match the Moon, how closely, and where does each match break?

We scored 22,147 pieces of Earth, 10 km each, against 13 places on the Moon.

  1. Score

    Same ground shape? Same rock? Rock counts 42%, shape 58%. A score of 1 is a perfect twin.

    Moon: NASA LRO, Kaguya. Earth: Copernicus DEM, GLiM rock map.
  2. Test

    Pair known Moon analogs with look-alikes that have the wrong rock. The AUC is how often the analog scores higher. 0.5 is a coin flip.

    15 known sites, 16 look-alikes
  3. Pick

    Skip the famous sites. A new place must be in the top 100 on two separate scores.

    65 robust candidates, at least 30 km apart
